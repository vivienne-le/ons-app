# Photo credits

All photos in `public/img/unsplash/` (event headers) and `public/img/album/` (shared-album seed photos) are free photos from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). The file name is the Unsplash photo id: `https://images.unsplash.com/photo-<id>`.

To swap an event photo, save it as `<photo id>-400.jpg` and `<photo id>-800.jpg` in `public/img/unsplash/` and change `photo:` for that event in `src/data/events.js`.
